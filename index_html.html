Welcome to our club

A Comfortable Space to Feel Stronger.

Ladies Fitness Club is built around a simple idea: women deserve a place where they can exercise comfortably, feel welcomed, and receive helpful guidance. With quality machines and a supportive atmosphere, we want every member to feel confident as she works towards her own goals.

Our journey began 11 years ago at a location near Attock Medical Centre. Today, we continue that commitment to women’s fitness in Attock.

The vision behind the club

A Passion for Helping Women Thrive.

Noreen Afzal founded Ladies Fitness Club with a clear vision: to create a place where women could share in her passion for fitness and train in a comfortable, encouraging environment. With more than 10 years of experience, she understands that every woman’s fitness journey is different—and that the right guidance, welcoming atmosphere, and reliable equipment can make the experience more positive.

What began as a vision has grown into a club serving women in Attock for 11 years. Her goal remains to help members feel at ease, learn at their own pace, and build habits that support a healthier life.

“I wanted to create a place where women could feel comfortable, supported, and inspired to make fitness part of their lives.”
